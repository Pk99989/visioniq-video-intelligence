# RAG evaluation — 2026-11-06

Dataset: `backend/tests/data/rag_eval.jsonl` — 54 questions (41 positive, 13 negative) over the three sample videos (bus 10 s, moving traffic 15 s, advanced 15 s, 2 scenes). Corpus captions by provider: {'ollama': 4} (VLM model moondream via Ollama). Correct hit = right video and timestamp within ±2 s. Run twice: once with hash embeddings (the default, no download) and once with FastEmbed bge-small-en-v1.5.

## What the numbers mean

- **Qdrant is a faithful accelerator:** A and C are identical on both embedding runs. An earlier candidate-pool bug (Qdrant pre-selecting by weak vectors) is fixed by fetching 100 candidates before hybrid scoring.
- **The reranker does not earn its latency here:** on hash it slightly hurts (R@1 0.37→0.32, 27× latency); on FastEmbed it slightly helps (0.27→0.32) but at 6–12× latency. It stays off by default (`RERANKER_MODEL` empty).
- **The agent's 0.45 evidence floor is tuned for hash embeddings.** With FastEmbed, unrelated text scores higher (all captions live in the same "video scene" region), so false evidence gets through and negatives correct falls 0.38→0.15. The floor needs per-provider calibration (measure known-negative score distributions, pick a floor with a margin) — open follow-up, deliberately not tuned to this 54-question set.
- **Precision@5 0.16 is a metric artefact of single-scene videos:** every frame in the moving-traffic video shares one caption, so the top 5 are near-duplicate frames and the ±2 s correctness window only credits ~1 of them. Recall@5 is the meaningful number here.
- Latency: hash search ~7 ms, FastEmbed ~36–68 ms, rerank adds ~190–420 ms per query on this CPU.
